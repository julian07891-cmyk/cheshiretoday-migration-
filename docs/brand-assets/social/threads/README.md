# Threads

This directory documents the approved native Threads publishing workflow.
Version 1 is deliberately text-led and has no dedicated production graphic
template. Existing approved Instagram or Facebook assets may be reused only
under the image rules below; a Threads-specific asset should be introduced only
when operational evidence establishes a need.

The authenticated Admin Social Publishing dialog now implements this preparation
contract as a transient text-only mode. It requires an explicit editorial
approval acknowledgement, a verified opening line and optional verified context,
then previews and copies the exact post ending with the canonical Cheshire Today
URL. It performs no backend generation, persistence, posting or scheduling.

## Version 1 publishing workflow

Threads Version 1 is a native, text-led editorial workflow. It does not require
a dedicated graphic generator or permanent template system.

### 1. Select and approve the story

1. Review current Cheshire Today articles and recommend the single strongest
   article for social publication.
2. Explain briefly why it is timely, useful and relevant to Cheshire readers.
3. Wait for editorial approval before writing final Threads copy.
4. After approval, prepare the final platform-specific post. Do not treat copy
   prepared for Facebook or Instagram as automatically approved for Threads.

Selection must support the rolling editorial mix:

- 40% Cheshire Local reporting;
- 40% Business, Finance, Property and Economy;
- 20% AI & Tech.

Apply the balance across a rolling set of posts rather than forcing a weak story
into an individual slot. Prefer reporting with direct Cheshire relevance,
public impact, economic value or a useful question for local readers. Do not use
routine crime, court or incident coverage as filler. Avoid clickbait, generic
national stories without a Cheshire consequence, promotional listicles and
posts selected only to maintain frequency.

### 2. Write the native post

Use this structure when it fits the story:

```text
Opening line: the clearest useful point, in natural language.

Context: one short verified sentence explaining why it matters in Cheshire.

Canonical Cheshire Today article URL
```

- Preserve the meaning of the approved article headline, but do not paste a
  formal headline when a more natural opening sentence reads better.
- Use only facts established by the published Cheshire Today article.
- Keep the post concise enough to scan without turning it into a headline-only
  link drop.
- Put the canonical Cheshire Today URL after the useful editorial context,
  normally as the final block.
- Never link to an Admin route, source-publisher URL, image URL, preference link
  or tokenised subscriber route.
- Do not add “breaking”, urgency or certainty beyond the published reporting.

### 3. Tone

Threads copy should be conversational, informed and locally grounded. Write as
an editor inviting a useful discussion, not as an automated promotion account.
Use plain English, short paragraphs and restrained punctuation. Avoid engagement
bait, exaggerated questions, unexplained acronyms, excessive emoji and repeated
calls to click.

### 4. Images and existing assets

Text plus the canonical link is the default treatment.

Include an image only when it adds editorial context or materially improves
recognition of the place, person, project or subject. Use the article's approved
featured image with its required attribution, or reuse an existing approved
Instagram Feed or Facebook asset only when:

- its crop is suitable for the Threads presentation;
- its text remains readable without platform-specific interface elements;
- its headline and category still match the article exactly;
- it contains no Instagram-only sticker instruction or Facebook-specific CTA;
- it passed its original export and editorial checks.

Do not stretch, recrop automatically or relabel an existing asset merely to make
it fit Threads. If the text post and link preview communicate the story clearly,
do not add a graphic.

### 5. Hashtags

Do not add hashtags by default. Threads posts should read as editorial
conversation rather than campaign copy. Use at most one specific hashtag only
when it has a clear discovery or live-event purpose. Do not copy Facebook or
Instagram hashtag blocks into Threads.

### 6. Replies and engagement

- Monitor replies after publication when operationally practical.
- Answer genuine questions only from verified reporting or the published
  article; link back to the relevant coverage when useful.
- Correct a material mistake clearly rather than silently arguing with a reader.
- Do not speculate, disclose unpublished information or repeat unverified claims.
- Do not amplify abusive, discriminatory or legally risky replies.
- Acknowledge useful local experience without presenting an anecdote as a
  verified fact.
- Escalate corrections, legal concerns and safety issues through the normal
  editorial process.

### 7. Frequency

Publish on a quality-led basis, normally one to three Threads posts on an active
news day. Fewer posts are preferable when no story meets the editorial standard.
Space posts so each has a clear purpose, and do not repeat the same article
unless there is a substantive verified update or a distinct reader question.

## Copy-ready formats

Replace each brace-delimited field only with verified material from the approved
article. Preserve the blank lines.

### Local News

```text
Five of Knutsford’s highest-rated independent restaurants on TripAdvisor.

A local guide to the independent places earning strong feedback from diners.

https://cheshiretoday.co.uk/article/6a65e9284730b1c10b2b37c0/five-of-knutsford-s-highest-rated-independent-restaurants-on-tripadvisor
```

### Business / Finance

```text
{Exact approved article headline}

{One verified sentence explaining the practical effect on Cheshire businesses,
workers, investors or households.}

{Canonical Cheshire Today article URL}
```

### AI & Tech

```text
{Exact approved article headline}

{One verified sentence explaining what the development means for Cheshire
people, organisations or the local economy.}

{Canonical Cheshire Today article URL}
```

### Question-led engagement

```text
{One specific, neutral question arising from the approved article}

Here is what the reporting establishes so far: {one short verified sentence}.

{Canonical Cheshire Today article URL}
```

Do not ask a question whose premise is unsupported by the article. Do not use a
question to manufacture controversy or invite speculation about crime, court,
health, personal or legally sensitive matters.

## Operational acceptance checklist

Before publishing, confirm:

- [ ] The article was recommended first and received editorial approval.
- [ ] The post supports the rolling 40/40/20 editorial mix without using filler.
- [ ] Every factual statement is supported by the published article.
- [ ] The opening is clear, conversational and free of clickbait.
- [ ] The canonical Cheshire Today URL is correct and contains no token or Admin
      path.
- [ ] The post contains no generic copied hashtag block.
- [ ] Any question is neutral, specific and supported by the reporting.
- [ ] Any image or reused asset adds context, has a suitable crop and retains
      required attribution.
- [ ] No Instagram-only or Facebook-specific instruction remains in reused copy
      or artwork.
- [ ] The post has been previewed in Threads and is ready for a final editor
      check before publication.

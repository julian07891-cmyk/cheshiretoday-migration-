# Cheshire Today Brand Guidelines v1.0

## 1. Purpose

Cheshire Today is a trusted, accessible digital publication for people who live, work and invest in Cheshire. Its identity must support clear reporting, confident editorial judgement and a clean reader experience across the website, newsletters, social channels and commercial material.

The publication is local first. Cheshire reporting is the foundation of the brand, supported by authoritative coverage of business, finance, property, the economy, AI and technology. National context should help readers understand its effect on Cheshire rather than displace local relevance.

Every brand application should feel editorial rather than application-like: informed, calm, useful and recognisably connected to the production website.

## 2. Brand principles

### Trust

Present information with restraint. Use approved assets consistently, distinguish editorial content from promotion and never use urgent visual treatment to exaggerate routine stories.

### Accuracy

Headlines, quotations, figures, captions, credits and calls to action must accurately represent the published article or campaign. Brand presentation must never imply facts that the underlying reporting does not establish.

### Clarity

Prioritise a clear headline hierarchy, readable typography, meaningful imagery and one obvious action. Remove decorative elements that compete with the story.

### Consistency

Use the production palette, typography and approved templates. Do not create platform-specific replacement identities, arbitrary category colours or unapproved logo variants.

### Accessibility

Design for legibility, sufficient contrast, clear language, safe margins and useful alternative text. Accessibility is part of editorial quality, not an optional finishing step.

## 3. Logo system

The existing Cheshire Today production logo treatment is authoritative until a separately reviewed logo-suite project is approved. Use the approved production master or export; do not redraw, trace, recolour or reconstruct it from unrelated type or icons.

### Primary logo

Use the complete Cheshire Today icon-and-wordmark lockup for website, newsletter, social and commercial applications where the available space permits. Preserve its original proportions and keep it level. Use a version with sufficient contrast against its background.

### Profile icon

Use the approved Cheshire Today profile symbol where a platform requires a compact square or circular avatar. Centre the symbol and preserve enough surrounding space for circular cropping. Do not substitute a letter, social-platform icon or Highlight symbol for the profile mark.

### Clear space

Keep clear space around the primary logo equal to at least the height of the small newspaper glyph within the mark. No headline, photograph edge, border, sponsor mark or platform control should enter that area. Give the profile icon at least one quarter of its visible width as clear space inside its export canvas.

### Minimum size

- Primary digital logo: do not display below `150 px` wide.
- Profile icon: do not display below `32 × 32 px`.
- At smaller sizes, use the approved profile icon instead of compressing the full wordmark.

Always inspect the final asset at its actual display size. If the wordmark or newspaper detail is no longer legible, use a larger size or the profile icon.

### Incorrect usage

Do not:

- stretch, squash, rotate, crop or separate parts of the primary lockup;
- recolour the logo with unapproved colours;
- add gradients, shadows, outlines, glow or other effects;
- place the logo over a visually busy or low-contrast area;
- put the logo inside an unapproved badge or shape;
- typeset a replacement wordmark;
- combine the logo with a sponsor mark without a reviewed co-branding layout.

## 4. Colour palette

All digital colour values are sRGB.

| Role | Value | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Royal Blue | `#1E3A8A` | Principal identity colour, mastheads, primary actions and brand fields |
| Royal Blue Hover | `#1B357D` | Darker interaction state or restrained tonal variant |
| Editorial Emerald | `#047857` | Editorial, civic, business and positive-impact accent |
| Editorial Emerald Action | `#059669` | Secondary actions, success treatment and editor guides |
| Breaking Red | `#DC2626` | Genuine urgent or breaking-news treatment only |
| Warm Paper | `#F7F4EE` | Main light editorial background |
| Warm Panel | `#FBFAF7` | Secondary surfaces, cards and image-placeholder fields |
| Warm Border | `#E6E1D8` | Dividers, frames and quiet structural lines |
| Primary Text | `#020617` | Editorial headlines on light backgrounds |
| Body Text | `#1E293B` | Main body and explanatory copy |
| Secondary Text | `#475569` | Metadata, captions and supporting copy |

Royal blue must remain the dominant identity colour. Emerald supports editorial distinction but must not replace royal blue as the master brand colour. Breaking red must never be used for routine crime, minor incidents, general promotion or decoration.

Warm paper and warm panel backgrounds should be preferred over stark white where the approved template provides them. Use warm border for separation rather than heavy boxes. Do not introduce arbitrary category palettes or generic platform blues.

## 5. Typography

### Approved families

- **Playfair Display** is the editorial headline family.
- **Public Sans** is the body, interface, label, metadata and call-to-action family.

Do not substitute Montserrat or make Inter the brand interface family. Email HTML may use documented email-safe fallbacks where client support requires them.

### Hierarchy

1. Primary editorial headline: Playfair Display, normally weight `700`.
2. Section or supporting headline: Playfair Display `600–700`, or Public Sans `700` where the treatment is functional rather than editorial.
3. Body copy: Public Sans `400–500`.
4. Labels, metadata and calls to action: Public Sans `600–700`.

### Headline rules

- Use natural title case rather than all capitals.
- Preserve the exact approved editorial headline unless a channel-specific version has been approved.
- Keep social headlines concise and normally within three lines.
- Adjust line breaks before reducing type size.
- Use comfortable line spacing and avoid tight letter spacing in Playfair Display.
- Do not use decorative type effects.

### Body-copy rules

- Use Public Sans with clear paragraph spacing and comfortable line length.
- Keep supporting copy concise and direct.
- Use sentence case for explanatory copy and calls to action.
- Avoid very light weights, dense blocks and excessive centred text.

### Accessibility

Use a practical minimum of `16 px` for ordinary digital body copy and test platform graphics at their expected phone-display size. Text must maintain sufficient contrast against its background and must not be embedded over a photograph without a reliable contrasting field.

## 6. Social asset library

The canonical social systems are held under `docs/brand-assets/social/`:

- Instagram Highlight Suite v1.0 in `highlights/`;
- Instagram Story Template System v1.0 in `stories/templates/`;
- Instagram Feed Template System v1.0 in `feed/templates/`;
- Reels Cover Template System v1.0 in `reels/templates/`;
- Facebook Template System v1.0 in `facebook/templates/`;
- native Threads publishing workflow v1.0 in `threads/README.md`.

### SVG-first workflow

1. Duplicate the appropriate approved SVG master.
2. Replace every visible placeholder with approved editorial content.
3. Preserve the master grid, safe area, typography and palette.
4. Show hidden guides only while editing and hide them before export.
5. Validate XML, IDs, accessibility references, dimensions and internal asset references.
6. Review at full size and expected mobile-display size.
7. Export the approved working master to sRGB PNG.
8. Confirm that no placeholder copy, editor guide, external reference or tracking data remains.

Never overwrite a published Version 1.0 master. Permanent masters remain free of photographs, external resources, scripts and tracking data.

### PNG export standards

- Highlight covers: `1080 × 1080 px`.
- Square Feed graphics: `1080 × 1080 px`.
- Stories and Reels: `1080 × 1920 px`.
- A future portrait Threads graphic: `1080 × 1350 px` only when separately approved.
- Facebook landscape graphics: `1200 × 630 px`.

Export in sRGB at the exact master dimensions. Do not upscale a smaller raster file. Use the same base filename for an SVG working asset and its PNG export.

### Safe areas

- Highlights: central `560 × 560 px` circular-crop safe area.
- Stories and Reels: `72 px` side margins, `250 px` top margin and `300 px` bottom margin; critical area `x=72–1008`, `y=250–1620`.
- Square Feed graphics: `72 px` on every side; critical area `x=72–1008`, `y=72–1008`.

### Naming conventions

Use lowercase kebab-case. Do not use spaces, dates, editor names, `final` or `new`. Stable production masters retain their approved filenames. New public versions require an explicit version suffix or versioned directory.

## 7. Editorial imagery

### Photography

Use relevant, accurate photography that supports the reported story. Prefer clear local context, identifiable places, people carrying out the reported activity and images with a strong editorial focal point. Do not use unrelated stock imagery where a genuine source image exists.

### Cropping

Preserve the photograph's editorial meaning. Keep faces, landmarks, signs and essential context inside the crop. Do not force every image into an identical composition, distort its proportions or crop away information needed to understand the story. Check crops at both full and thumbnail sizes.

### Image quality

Use the strongest legitimate source available. Images must be sharp enough for the target export and free from broken, generic or low-value placeholders. Do not upscale visibly poor source material or conceal quality problems with effects.

### AI-generated artwork

Do not use AI-generated artwork as a substitute for documentary photography of reported people, places or events. Any exceptional illustrative use requires separate editorial approval, clear labelling and a context where readers cannot mistake it for evidence. AI artwork must never be added to permanent template masters.

### Attribution

Retain required photographer, publisher, agency and supplied-image credits. Do not remove embedded attribution that is part of the licensed source image. Captions and credits must be accurate, readable and separated from promotional copy.

## 8. Social publishing standards

### Instagram

Use an approved Feed, Story, Reel or Highlight master. The authenticated Admin Social Publishing dialog supports the approved Top Story, Local News Feed and Local News Reels Cover workflows, including exact PNG export and deterministic format-specific copy. Keep the graphic concise and place full context in the caption. Add platform alt text and review both the full post and profile-grid crop before publication. No Admin preparation action publishes or schedules content.

### Facebook

Use the approved Facebook Template System or the authenticated Admin Facebook Publishing Studio. Preserve accurate headlines, meaningful images, clear link context and the documented `1200 × 630 px` output. Use Link Preview for native link sharing and Branded Graphic when an approved graphic treatment adds value.

### Threads

Follow the native Threads workflow in `social/threads/README.md`: lead with clear conversational editorial text and use graphics only where they add context. The Admin Social Publishing dialog provides transient editor-approved post preparation and copying, with no graphic generation, persistence, posting or scheduling. No dedicated Threads template is currently required. Do not repurpose an asset whose crop or text hierarchy is unsuitable, and preserve the same voice and attribution standards.

### Reels

Use an approved Reel cover master and keep the logo, category, headline, Reel badge and CTA inside the shared safe area. Verify the central profile-grid crop as well as the full-screen cover. The cover must accurately describe the Reel.

### Stories

Use an approved Story master. Keep platform link, poll or interactive stickers inside the safe area without covering the logo or headline. Place link stickers above the bottom interface region and confirm legibility on a phone.

Across every channel, reserve breaking treatment for genuinely urgent news. Do not present routine promotion or minor incidents as breaking news.

## 9. Accessibility

### Colour contrast

Use established high-contrast production combinations: dark text on warm paper or warm panel, and warm-paper text on royal blue, emerald or breaking red. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate category, urgency or state.

### Alternative text

Write concise alt text describing the meaningful content and context of the finished graphic. Do not repeat the full caption or start with “image of”. Decorative elements need no separate description. SVG masters must retain valid `<title>` and `<desc>` accessibility metadata.

### Readable typography

Check every asset at its expected mobile size. Avoid condensed letter spacing, overly long all-capital labels, low-contrast text and more than three headline lines. Use the approved family fallbacks only where the delivery channel requires them.

### Safe margins

Keep all essential content within the documented template safe area. Account for platform interface overlays and circular or profile-grid crops. Hidden guides are editing tools and must never appear in a public export.

## 10. Version control

The canonical Brand Asset Library is:

```text
docs/brand-assets/
```

Approved SVG masters and their documented exports are production assets. Once an asset has been used publicly, treat that version as immutable.

- Use semantic document and system versions such as `v1.0` for the first approved release, `v1.1` for compatible corrections and `v2.0` for a materially changed system.
- Do not silently overwrite a public master.
- Record approval status, intended channels, validation and replacement details in the nearest README or operational project state.
- Keep drafts outside permanent production directories until reviewed.
- Validate dimensions, XML, colour, typography, accessibility, safe areas and export parity before approval.
- Website runtime assets remain in their existing production locations unless a separate implementation explicitly integrates a library asset.

Future changes require focused review against the live website, the current Brand Asset Library and these guidelines. Production deployment is a separate decision from asset approval.

# Cheshire Today – Welcome Email Production Checklist

**Preview file:** `/tmp/welcome_email_preview.html`  
**Subject:** *Welcome to Cheshire Today! 📰 Your Local News Awaits*  

---

## 1. Header & Branding
- [ ] Logo is visible and correctly linked.
- [ ] Header text: “Welcome to the Family! Your trusted source for Cheshire news.”
- [ ] Background color matches brand palette (blue).
- [ ] Header and logo are responsive on mobile devices.

## 2. Main Welcome Copy
- [ ] Friendly welcome message present and correctly spelled.
- [ ] Paragraph spacing ensures readability on desktop and mobile.

## 3. Newsletter Types / What to Expect
- [ ] **The Daily Brief** – Top Cheshire stories at 7:30 AM.
- [ ] **Weekly Roundup** – Week’s best stories every Sunday at 9:00 AM.
- [ ] **Breaking News** – Urgent alerts for major local stories.
- [ ] Icons/emojis are displayed correctly and do not break layout.

## 4. Coverage Area
- [ ] All relevant towns listed correctly.
- [ ] Background color distinct for visual separation (green).
- [ ] Text does not overflow container on smaller screens.

## 5. Call-to-Action
- [ ] “Read Latest News →” button prominent and clearly visible.
- [ ] URL points to homepage or featured news section.
- [ ] Button is clickable on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Button color contrasts with background for accessibility.

## 6. Social Links
- [ ] Facebook and Twitter links present.
- [ ] Icons render correctly.
- [ ] Links open in a new tab/window.

## 7. Preferences & Unsubscribe
- [ ] “Preferences” link present and personalized per subscriber.
- [ ] “Unsubscribe” link present and functional.
- [ ] Links clearly visible and accessible.

## 8. Footer
- [ ] Copyright present: “© 2026 Cheshire Today. All rights reserved.”
- [ ] Legal/compliance text included where necessary.

## 9. HTML & Rendering
- [ ] No broken HTML tags.
- [ ] Images display correctly; fallback `alt` text included.
- [ ] Email scales correctly on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- [ ] Duplicate links such as “Read More →” removed.

## 10. Technical / Tracking
- [ ] Tracked URLs implemented for Preferences and Unsubscribe.
- [ ] Embedded tracking pixels or UTMs ready for analytics.

---

**Notes:**  
- Ensure AI/Tech, Business/Finance, Local, and National sections are correctly assigned.  
- Confirm that no banned categories (Sports, Entertainment, Celebrity, Gaming) appear.  
- Validate preview in multiple clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, mobile).
